How this estimate works

This calculator uses real-world data from parking operations across the country to estimate what your lot could generate. Enter your number of spaces, average occupancy, hourly rate, and typical parking duration to see projected monthly and annual revenue.

The base estimate reflects what a standard Scan-to-Pay setup can produce. The LPR enhancement estimate shows the additional revenue gained through license plate recognition technology, which improves compliance rates by catching unpaid parkers automatically.

Every property is different. A 30-space church lot earning weekday revenue will look different from a 200-space event venue charging premium rates on game nights. That is why we offer free, no-obligation consultations to walk through your specific situation and recommend the right approach for your property type, location, and goals.

Your actual parking lot revenue depends on factors like local demand, nearby competition, signage, and pricing strategy. This estimate gives you a reliable starting point to decide whether parking monetization makes sense for your property.
