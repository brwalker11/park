Empty spaces become income

Private lots often sit empty during off-hours while nearby demand goes unmet. The right technology captures that demand and turns it into monthly income.

License plate recognition and enforcement

Payment technology only collects from people who choose to pay. Compliance is what decides the number at the end of the month, and compliance comes from enforcement rather than from the payment method. License plate recognition, or LPR, reads plates against paid sessions automatically, so there are no gates, no kiosks, and no attendant. Whether a lot needs it, and which system fits, is part of what the consultation answers.

No product line to protect

Most companies offering parking technology sell parking technology, so the recommendation and the product are the same thing. This is consulting.
